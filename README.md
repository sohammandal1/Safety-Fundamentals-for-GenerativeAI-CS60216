# Safety Fundamentals for Generative AI (CS60216) - Course Resources

Hello Everyone! 👋

I am **Soham Mandal**, a recent graduate of **IIT Kharagpur** from the **Deparrtment of Electrical Engineering**.

This repository contains the materials, notes, assignments, implementations, and other resources I used during the course **Safety Fundamentals for Generative AI (CS60216)**.

The course focuses on understanding the **safety and alignment aspects of Generative AI systems**, with particular emphasis on Large Language Models (LLMs), their training mechanisms, fine-tuning approaches, and techniques for building reliable safety guardrails.

## 📚 Course Focus

The repository explores topics including:

- **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and their training mechanisms
- **Pre-training and post-training** of generative models
- **Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)**
- **Instruction tuning and alignment**
- **Preference optimization and RL-based alignment**
- **LLM safety and alignment challenges**
- **Jailbreaks and adversarial prompting**
- **Safety evaluation and red-teaming**
- **Guardrail mechanisms for Generative AI**
- **Content moderation and safety filtering**
- **Model behavior and refusal mechanisms**
- **Robustness and reliability of LLMs**
- **Responsible and trustworthy Generative AI**

## 🎯 Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to serve as a helpful resource for:

- Students planning to take **CS60216** in upcoming semesters.
- Students interested in **LLM safety, alignment, and responsible AI**.
- Anyone looking to understand how **training and fine-tuning influence model behavior**.
- Enthusiasts exploring **safety guardrails and evaluation techniques for Generative AI**.
- Anyone interested in experimenting with the practical aspects of **LLM safety and alignment**.
